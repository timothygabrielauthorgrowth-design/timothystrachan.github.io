Editorial notes / manuscript
Start with the actual book.
The starting point

Specific attention
comes first.

We don't begin with generic praise or a list of services. We begin by understanding the book, the author behind it, and where the distance between the book's quality and its visibility actually sits.

That means reading closely, noticing what is distinctive, and being honest about what could be working better. The marketing follows from that understanding.

How we work

Three steps, without
the theatre.

01

Read closely.

We spend time with the book before suggesting anything. We look at its subject, voice, audience and the details that make it itself.

02

Find the real gap.

We work out where the problem actually is — whether that is discoverability, positioning, reaching the right readers, or something more specific.

03

Build momentum.

The aim is not a brief spike followed by silence. We focus on useful, repeatable work that can give a book a longer life and a clearer path to its readers.

A useful distinction

Who this is — and isn't — for.

A good fit if...

  • You have a finished or published book you care about.
  • You want marketing grounded in the actual book.
  • You are interested in building visibility over time.
  • You value a direct, personal working relationship.
  • You are open to hearing what may need to change.

Probably not a fit if...

  • You are looking for guaranteed sales or rankings.
  • You want a one-week publicity shortcut.
  • You want a generic package without discussion.
  • You are primarily looking for paid advertising alone.
  • You don't want anyone looking closely at the book first.

Gabriel & Strachan is a real, currently small book marketing practice run by Timothy Gabriel and Sue Strachan. We are not presenting ourselves as a large agency, and we don't have a catalogue of famous authors to put on this page.
